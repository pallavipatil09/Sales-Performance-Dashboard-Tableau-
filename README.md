## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Tableau to analyze business sales, profit trends, customer segments, and top-selling products. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI reporting.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Businesses often struggle to monitor sales performance, profitability, and customer purchasing behavior efficiently. This dashboard helps stakeholders track key business metrics, identify high-performing products, and make data-driven decisions.

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- Sales by Region Analysis
- Top Selling Products Visualization
- Total Sales vs Profit vs Profit Margin Trends
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Sales Difference Before/After Discount
- KPI Cards for Profit Tracking
- Interactive Dashboard Design

- Total Sales
- Profit Margin
- Regional Sales Performance
- Top Products
- Customer Segment Contribution

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Identified top-performing products contributing the highest revenue.
- Analyzed regional sales distribution across different markets.
- Observed increasing sales and profit trends over multiple years.
- Evaluated the impact of discounts on product sales.
